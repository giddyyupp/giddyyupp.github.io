Samet Hicsonmez

I am a Reseach Associate in CVI2 Lab at University of Luxembourg. I received my Ph.D. in the Computer Engineering Department at Hacettepe University under supervision of Prof. Dr. Pinar Duygulu and Asst. Prof. Emre Akbas.
Before coming to Hacettepe, I received my master degree from TOBB Economics and Technology University, Computer Engineering Department in 2013, and bachelor degree from Istanbul Technical University, Electronics Engineering in 2009.
My primary research interests lie in computer vision, with a focus on generative models, 2D/3D object detection and segmentation.
